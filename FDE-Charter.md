# Charter — The Forward-Deployed CMO

> The single sentence everything reports to. Read this before every session.

---

## North star

**Scott Stern is building the rarest profile in enterprise SaaS marketing leadership: a future-ready CMO who can personally stand up the agentic marketing system *and* scale the teams that run it — backed by a documented business case that makes him impossible to overlook for the next chapter.**

This is not a study project. It is the deliberate construction of a career thesis, executed in public, while continuing to lead at BlackLine.

---

## The differentiation spine (the four-way intersection)

No one else credibly holds all four at once. This is the moat.

1. **Forward-looking executive with fingers on the keyboard** — doesn't commission the system, builds it; doesn't approve the prototype, ships it.
2. **Knows how to set up the systems** — MCP architecture, agentic workflows, skills, evals, orchestration — and how to transfer them to a team.
3. **Technically superior in marketing** — 20+ years of enterprise SaaS marketing depth: PMM, demand, content ops, analyst relations, customer adoption, GTM.
4. **Corporate finance fluency + IPO-scale SaaS growth + category narrative** — speaks CFO/CRO as a peer, has scaled marketing 8→80 through a ~$5B IPO, and creates categories (OneStream CPM, Workiva platform, BlackLine Agentic Financial Operations).

The first two are what's being built over the next 90 days. The last two already exist. The combination is the future-ready CMO.

---

## What this is

- A **wedge into bigger leadership**, not an exit ramp out of it. The endgame is a CMO seat earned by being the executive who *does the work and scales teams to do the work*.
- **Additive to BlackLine.** This is the proof case for the exact thesis Scott markets — agentic operations, lived. It strengthens the day job; it does not compete with it.
- **Learn-and-publish simultaneously.** Every capability built becomes visible proof. The brand is constructed in the background of the learning, not after it.

## What this is NOT

- Not tool-tinkering. If it doesn't ship and a real marketing team couldn't use it, it doesn't count.
- Not a pivot to engineering. The title stays executive. "Engineer" is an operating model, not a job target.
- Not separate from the career. This *is* the career trajectory, accelerated.

---

## Non-negotiables

1. **Something ships every week.** Visible. In the repo. Catalogued. Knowledge that can't be pointed to does not count.
2. **The business case sharpens every Friday.** One section, every week. It is a living document, not a Day-90 write-up.
3. **BlackLine first.** This runs in the margins of the day job, never through it. Nothing BlackLine-confidential becomes a public artifact.

---

## The endgame, stated plainly

By Day 90: 4–5 deployable artifacts, a codified delivery method, a public POV thread, and a business case sharp enough to put in front of leadership that says — *here is an executive who saw where the market was going, built the capability himself, proved it in public, and can now scale a team to do it across the org.*

That is the future-ready CMO. This charter is the contract to become one.
